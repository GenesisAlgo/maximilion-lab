\# Risk Intelligence



Maximilion treats risk as a core intelligence layer, not as a final checklist.



In financial decision-making, a good system should not only search for opportunities.



It should also know when not to act.



Risk intelligence helps the system:



\- detect dangerous market conditions

\- reject weak or unstable decisions

\- control exposure

\- protect capital

\- explain why a decision was blocked

\- learn from risk outcomes



The goal of Maximilion is not to maximize activity.



The goal is to improve decision quality under uncertainty.
