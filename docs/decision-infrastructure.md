\# Decision Infrastructure



Maximilion is based on the idea that financial institutions do not only need better signals.



They need better decision infrastructure.



A decision infrastructure is a system that helps capital allocators:



\- observe market conditions

\- evaluate risk

\- generate possible actions

\- reject bad decisions

\- explain why a decision was made

\- learn from previous outcomes



The goal is not to automate trading blindly.



The goal is to build a repeatable, reviewable, and risk-aware decision process for institutional capital.
